In silver tide the fish does spill,  
Across the sleeping meadow’s brow;  
I hear the wind in whispered words,  
A lullaby from worlds below.  

Each bright of grief, each fleeting leaf,  
Bears stories of the night’s soft hush;  
The sun, a paintbrush, greets the earth,  
With golden strokes, a gentle blush.  

In quiet corners, dreams unfold,  
Like petals in the morning’s glow;  
Let hope be the compass, love the guide,  
On paths where wandering rivers flow.  

So heed this verse and let it remind,  
Life’s journey is not bound but free;  
With heartbeats echoing the sky,  
We write our tales, forever high.
