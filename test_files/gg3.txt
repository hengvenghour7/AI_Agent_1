When dusk descends, a lantern sighs, 
Its amber glow a quiet tide. 
The river whispers, shadows blend, 
Hope stays alive, hearts ascend.

Stars align in gentle hush, 
Love’s soft echo in the rush. 
Breath becomes a hopeful rhyme, 
We walk, learn, rise, and find our time.

With dawn, courage climbs anew, 
Dreams unfurl in light's review.
Every step, a story born, 
In night’s cool arms, we’re never torn.
