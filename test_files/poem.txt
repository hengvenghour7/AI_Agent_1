In twilight's hush the stars begin,
A quiet breath, a gentle spin.
The moon alights upon night's face,
Its silver light a tender lace.

Beneath this glow, my heart does roam,
Seeking solace far from home.
Each whispered wind recalls a dream,
Where hope and wonder intertwine gleam.

So let us walk in shadows soft,
Hand in hand, no fear aloft.
Together we shall find our way
To dawn's bright promise at the day.

---
New dawn awakes with gentle light,
A world reborn from darkest night.
The breeze carries songs of hope so clear,
In every breath, a brand-new cheer.

Rising with dawn, the sky hums,
A silver thread of hope begins.
Whispers of wind in rustling trees,
Echoes that dance upon the breeze.
