In twilight’s hush a lone lantern glows,
Soft amber light where the river flows.
Shadows stretch, yet hope is still awake,
Guiding wandering hearts that dare to make.

The stars align with quiet grace,
Whispering of love, a warm embrace.
Each breath a promise, every sigh a song,
In the night’s cool arms, we all belong.

May courage rise with dawn’s first gleam,
Dreams unfold in a hopeful stream.
We walk, we learn, we rise, we fall,
Yet in the end, we hear the call.
