A wandering heart, a silent song,
Under moonlit skies where dreams belong.
Stardust whispers in the night’s embrace,
Finding solace in every trace.

Leaves dance in a gentle breeze,
Carving memories with quiet ease.
Footsteps echo through the years,
Echoes fade, but hope appears.

The horizon calls with distant light,
Guiding wanderers through the night.
May courage bloom in every soul,
And love’s bright fire forever roll.
